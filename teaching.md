# Teaching & Mentoring

My teaching interests span the intersection of artificial intelligence, systems, security, and privacy. I am especially interested in courses that help students connect core technical foundations with modern AI systems and real deployment constraints.

## Courses I Am Interested in Teaching

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Computer and Network Security
- Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning
- Applied Cryptography / Secure Computation
- Efficient AI and ML Systems
- Deep Learning
- Computer Systems

## Mentoring

I value research mentoring that gives students both technical depth and increasing independence. In research projects, I emphasize clear problem formulation, reproducible experimentation, careful comparison with prior work, and the ability to explain why a result matters beyond a single benchmark.

I am particularly interested in mentoring students who want to work across traditional boundaries between machine learning, systems, and security.
